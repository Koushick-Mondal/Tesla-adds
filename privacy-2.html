Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Summary

Roadster Loop runs entirely in your browser. We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, email or location.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your best score in your browser's local storage under the key roadsterLoopBest. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Third-party resources

The game loads the three.js library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Your browser contacts that server to download it, so Cloudflare may receive your IP address and standard request data under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

This game does not currently display ads. If advertising is added in the future, ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and this page will be updated with details and choices available to you.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Trademark notice

Roadster Loop is an unofficial fan game and is not affiliated with Tesla. The car design and branding in the game are original.
